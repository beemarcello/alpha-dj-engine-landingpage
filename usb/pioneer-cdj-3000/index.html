What Alpha writes for the CDJ-3000

Alpha writes a full rekordbox USB the player reads directly: the classic export.pdb library every CDJ and XDJ has read for years, the newer encrypted exportLibrary.db the latest players prefer, the per-track analysis files with beat grid, cues and waveforms, album artwork, and the audio under Contents/<Artist>/<Album>. No rekordbox software is needed anywhere in the chain — the stick plugs straight into the gear.

The beat grid is built from the interpreted BPM, so tracks arrive beat-ready and the player does not re-analyze anything. One limit to know: Alpha writes one constant beat grid per track, so tempo changes inside a track are not modelled.

The CDJ-3000 is the newest generation of the club standard and prefers the encrypted exportLibrary.db — which Alpha writes alongside the classic export.pdb, so the stick works on the 3000 and on every older player in the same booth. Your playlists show up in the browse screen exactly as sequenced.

From folder to CDJ-3000 in four steps

  1. Point Alpha at your music — whole folders, hand-picked files, or a rekordbox XML export if you want to keep the BPM, key and beatgrid rekordbox already worked out.
  2. Pick the energy arc your night should follow — Journey, Wave, Build, Peak-time or Cooldown, or drag the curve yourself — set the length, and Alpha orders the tracks so neighboring songs actually mix.
  3. Audition the set in the built-in player and drag any track to a new position until the order feels right.
  4. Plug in your stick, choose Pioneer DJ / AlphaTheta as the target and click Write to USB. Alpha writes both rekordbox libraries plus the analysis files, validates the result — then eject and load the playlist on the CDJ-3000.

Good to know on the CDJ-3000

  • Alpha writes both rekordbox libraries — the classic export.pdb and the newer encrypted exportLibrary.db the CDJ-3000 prefers — so one stick serves new and old players alike.

Common questions about the CDJ-3000

Do I need rekordbox or a subscription to play Alpha's stick on a CDJ-3000?

No. The CDJ-3000 reads the rekordbox database from the stick itself, and Alpha writes that database directly — no rekordbox software, account or subscription anywhere in the chain.

Will the CDJ-3000 re-analyze my tracks?

No — BPM, beat grid, waveforms and cues are in the ANLZ analysis files Alpha writes, so tracks are ready the moment they load.

Can I use the CDJ-3000's SD card slot?

Yes — the CDJ-3000 reads the same rekordbox library from USB stick or SD card. Point Alpha's export at whichever is mounted on your Mac.

Which filesystem should the stick use — FAT32 or exFAT?

Both work on the CDJ-3000: it reads FAT32 and HFS+, and exFAT since firmware 1.20. If the same stick must also play on older players like the CDJ-2000NXS2, stay on FAT32. Alpha never reformats your drive.
