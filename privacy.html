Your account

Using Alpha requires an account. We store your email address, a username and your password. The username does not have to be your real name — pick whatever you like. Passwords are never stored in plain text; we keep only a salted hash.

The account starts on this website: when you click a download button, you enter your email address and confirm it with a six-digit code we send you. Only after that confirmation does the download begin. You choose your username and password later, the first time you open Alpha.

This sign-up runs on Clerk (Clerk.com, Inc., San Francisco, USA) as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Clerk receives your email address, the confirmation code, your IP address and your browser's user agent, and sets cookies needed to keep you signed in. The transfer to the USA is safeguarded by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Art. 46 GDPR.

Clerk's software is loaded only when you actually open the download dialog — not when you simply browse this site. If you never click a download button, no data reaches Clerk.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract and steps taken at your request before entering into one.

Licence and devices

We store your licence key together with an identifier for each device you activate. We need this to honour the licence terms — one person, one device — and to let you move your licence to a new machine.

While you are on the free tier, we also count how many tracks you have analyzed, up to the limit of ten. After that the counter stops mattering: the paid licence is unlimited and permanent.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract.

What the app sends us

Your audio files stay on your machine. Tempo, key and energy are computed locally, and no audio is ever transmitted.

Arranging your set is done on our servers. For that, the app sends the metadata of the tracks in your set — such as title, artist, tempo, key, energy and duration — so the sequencing engine can order them. This is what makes the harmonic arrangement work.

Writing the USB drive is local as well — the drive is plugged into your machine, not ours.

The app contains no crash reporting and no usage analytics. It does not report back what you do with it.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract.

How long we keep your account

Because the licence is permanent, we keep your account data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete it at any time and we will, subject only to invoices and accounting records, which German tax and commercial law (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB) requires us to retain for up to ten years.

This website

The site consists of static pages and loads nothing from anyone else — fonts, stylesheets, scripts and icons all come from this domain. No request goes to Google Fonts or to any content delivery network.

Hosting is provided by Hetzner Online GmbH, in a data centre in Germany, as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Their servers keep the usual access logs — your IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, the referring page and your browser's user agent. We use these only to operate the site securely and to investigate faults.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in a working, secure website.

Cookies and analytics

The site itself sets no cookies of its own.

We use Google Analytics 4, a service of Google Ireland Ltd., to understand how the site is used. It sets cookies and processes usage data, including a shortened form of your IP address. This happens only if you consent; you can withdraw that consent at any time with effect for the future. Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, safeguarded by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG — your consent.

Email addresses you give us

There are two different reasons we might have your address, and they follow different rules.

To download Alpha. Your address becomes your account name, so we need it to give you the software and your free tracks. We confirm it with a six-digit code before the download starts, and we send you the download link afterwards as a backup in case the download fails or you switch machines. This is not marketing, and it does not depend on your consent — it is what you asked us to do. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

To tell you about Alpha. If you ask to hear about new releases, or leave your address to be told when your device is supported, we confirm it first: you receive a message with a confirmation link, and we only add your address once you have clicked it (double opt-in). We record the time of your confirmation so we can demonstrate that you gave it. Every message contains an unsubscribe link, and you can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — your consent.

We do not pass your address on. We keep it for as long as you have an account, or until you unsubscribe. After that we retain the record of your consent for up to three years, so we can show it was given if that is ever questioned.

Emails are sent through Twilio SendGrid as our processor under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). The confirmation code itself is sent by Clerk (see “Your account”). Where processing takes place outside the EU, it is safeguarded by standard contractual clauses.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your payment details directly with Stripe; they are not visible to us. We receive only what we need to issue your licence and your invoice.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract.

How we share data

We do not sell your data. We share it only with processors bound by a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR, with public authorities where we are legally required to, and otherwise only with your explicit consent.

Transfers outside the EU

Where a processor operates outside the EU, the transfer is safeguarded by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Art. 46 GDPR.

Data security

We use TLS encryption for data in transit, role-based access control with multi-factor authentication on our own systems, separated development and production environments, and regular backups.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data (Art. 15), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise any of these, write to info@alphabees.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — for us that is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

Children

Alpha is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.
