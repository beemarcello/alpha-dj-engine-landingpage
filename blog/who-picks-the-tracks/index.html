Who picks the tracks: you or Alpha?

Tell a DJ that software builds their set and the conversation usually ends there — and fairly so, because the set is the job. So here is the split, control by control.

What you set before Alpha does anything

Nothing is generated until three decisions are made, and all three are yours.

  • The shape of the night. You pick an energy arc template — Journey, Wave, Build, Peak-time or Cooldown — and then drag its points to bend the curve into whatever your room actually needs.
  • The length. One hour by default, adjusted in ten-minute steps. Fit all tracks sizes the set to everything you imported.
  • How it weighs things. Two sliders, both centred until you move them. The first runs between harmonic mixing and following your drawn curve; the app puts it plainly in its own tooltip: “Pull toward Harmonic for the smoothest key transitions between tracks; pull toward Energy arc to follow your drawn energy curve more closely, even if some key changes are rougher.” The second decides whether neighbouring tracks are matched more on key or on tempo.

What Alpha decides

One thing: the order. It takes the tracks it analyzed and arranges them so the sequence follows the curve you drew, weighted by the two sliders you set. Up to a thousand tracks in a set. That is the entire extent of the machine's judgement — an order, proposed.

What you do with that order

The generated set opens in Refine as a row of energy bars with a mini-player. Click a bar to load that track, play it, scrub it. Drag a bar left or right to move the track. Remove anything that does not belong. The hint under the set reads “drag bars to fine-tune”, which is the honest description of the step.

And if your playlist is already in exactly the order you want, the sequencer can be skipped outright: Send to Export without composing → at the top right of the Compose step sends every analyzed track to Export in its current order. Alpha then does no ordering at all.

What Alpha never touches

  • Your audio files. Nothing is rewritten, nothing is re-encoded.
  • A track's key or tempo. Alpha orders tracks; it does not change them.
  • Your library on disk. Removing a track in Refine takes it out of this set — the file stays where it is.

The short version

Alpha does the part of the evening nobody misses: reading every file, finding key and tempo, proposing a first order, and writing the stick in the format your player reads natively. Then the USB comes out, goes into your gear, and the set is yours to play.

The first ten tracks are free — the full app, no demo mode, no card. Alpha runs on macOS and Windows.
